The Capsule Wardrobe Formula: 30 Pieces, 100+ Outfits

A simple formula for a capsule wardrobe that actually works: palette first, then a category ratio, then a seasonal swap.

By the All-Style editors · 6 min read · Updated November 26, 2026

In this guide
  1. What a capsule wardrobe is
  2. Start with palette, not items
  3. The category ratio
  4. Build it for your real week
  5. Season swaps and maintenance

What a capsule wardrobe is

A capsule wardrobe is a small set of interchangeable pieces (commonly 25 to 40, excluding underwear, sportswear and sleepwear) chosen so nearly everything combines. The payoff is fewer decisions, less spending and a more consistent look.

Start with palette, not items

Pick one base neutral (black, navy, charcoal or camel), two supporting neutrals (white, cream, grey, denim) and one or two accent colours. If every item uses these, the maths of combinations works in your favour. Your colour season can guide the accents.

The category ratio

A balanced 30-piece capsule might be: 10 tops, 6 bottoms, 3 dresses or jumpsuits (or 3 extra tops), 4 layers (blazer, knit, jacket, coat), 4 pairs of shoes and 3 bags or key accessories. With 10 tops and 6 bottoms alone you have 60 combinations before adding layers.

Build it for your real week

Weight the capsule toward where you spend time. An office-heavy week needs more tailored trousers and knits; a creative or remote week needs elevated casual pieces. The Capsule Wardrobe Builder adjusts the list to your lifestyle, season and size of capsule, and saves your progress on your device.

Season swaps and maintenance

Keep around 70% of pieces year-round and swap the rest each season (linen for wool, sandals for boots). Before buying anything new, ask: does it match my palette, does it combine with three items I own, and would I buy it at full price? Then try the Outfit Shuffler to discover combinations you've been missing.

FAQ

How many items should a capsule wardrobe have?

Most people land between 25 and 40 pieces. Fewer is possible but needs very consistent laundry and a tight palette.

Does a capsule wardrobe have to be neutral?

No. It needs a consistent palette, which can include bright accent colours as long as they work with your neutrals.

Is a capsule wardrobe cheaper?

Usually, over time. You may spend more per item, but you buy far fewer items and wear each more often.
