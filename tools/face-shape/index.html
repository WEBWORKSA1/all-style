Face Shape Finder

Measure your forehead, cheekbones, jaw and face length to identify your face shape, then see haircuts and glasses that create balance.

Use the same unit (cm or in) for every measurement.

How it works

The finder compares your face length with your cheekbone width and checks which of the three widths dominates. Long faces (ratio above about 1.55) are oval or oblong; short ratios (below about 1.3) are round or square; a wide forehead with a pointed chin suggests heart, and dominant cheekbones with a narrow forehead and jaw suggest diamond. FAQ

Can I use a photo instead of a tape?

Yes. Take a straight-on photo with your hair pulled back and measure on screen; ratios matter, not absolute numbers.

Does face shape matter for men's haircuts?

Yes. Men adjust with the height of the top, the fade height and beard shape to balance the face.

What matters more, face shape or hair texture?

Both. A cut that suits your face but fights your texture won't last; mention both to your stylist.
