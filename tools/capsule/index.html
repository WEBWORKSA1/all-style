Capsule Wardrobe Builder

Tell us how you live, choose a palette and a capsule size, and get a tailored checklist you can tick off as you shop. Your progress is saved on this device.

How it works

The builder balances categories using a proven ratio (roughly 10 tops : 6 bottoms : 4 layers : 4 shoes : accessories for a 30-piece capsule) and picks items for your lifestyle and season. Read the capsule wardrobe formula for the reasoning.

FAQ

Do underwear and sportswear count?

No. Most capsule methods exclude underwear, sleepwear, gym kit and special-occasion wear.

Can I add colour?

Yes. Pick an accent colour and repeat it in a few tops and accessories so they all combine.

Where is my checklist saved?

In your browser's local storage on this device only. Clearing site data resets it.
