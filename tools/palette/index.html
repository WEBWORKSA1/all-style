Palette Generator

Pick a colour you love and a harmony rule. The generator builds a five-colour palette with a 60-30-10 split, ready to copy as hex codes for paint, fabrics, outfits or wedding stationery.

How it works

The generator converts your colour to hue, saturation and lightness, then rotates the hue (30° for analogous, 180° for complementary, 120° for triadic) and adds a soft neutral and a deep anchor in the same hue family. The 60-30-10 rule assigns 60% to a dominant colour, 30% to a secondary and 10% to accents, a reliable balance in interiors and outfits.

FAQ

Will paint match the hex code exactly?

Screens and paints differ. Use the hex code to find the closest paint chip, then test a sample in your room's light.

Which harmony is safest?

Tonal and analogous schemes are the easiest to live with; complementary and triadic are bolder.

Can I use this for a wedding?

Yes. Use the soft neutral as the base, the start colour as the main colour and one accent sparingly.
