Colour Season Finder

Answer seven quick questions about your natural colouring and get your seasonal colour palette: the shades that make your skin look clearer and your eyes brighter, plus your best neutrals.

1. The veins on your inner wrist look mostly…
2. Which jewellery flatters you more?
3. Next to your face, which looks better?
4. Your natural hair colour (before greying or dye)
5. Your eye colour depth
6. Contrast between your hair, skin and eyes
7. Do your eyes look…

How it works

Seasonal colour analysis scores three dimensions: undertone (questions 1–3), depth (questions 4–5) and clarity (questions 6–7). Your strongest dimension decides your sub-season (for example Light, Deep, Bright, Soft, Warm or Cool), and your undertone decides the family (Spring, Summer, Autumn or Winter). FAQ

How accurate is an online colour analysis?

It's a reliable starting point when you answer in daylight without makeup. A professional draping session is more precise because it compares fabrics against your face in person.

Should I wear only my season's colours?

No. Use them near your face (tops, scarves, glasses, makeup). Trousers, shoes and bags can be anything you like.

Does hair dye change my season?

Dye changes your overall contrast, so some colours may flatter you more or less, but your skin's undertone stays the same.
