Makeover Budget Planner

Enter the budget you have and the room you're tackling. The planner allocates it across categories, sets aside a 10% contingency and, if you plan to hire help, reserves a design fee you choose.

0 if DIY. Use the fee your designer quotes.

How it works

The planner applies a room-specific split that puts most of the money into anchor pieces (sofa, bed, table, chair) that you'll use daily and keep longest, then lighting and rugs, which change a room the most per dollar, and keeps 10% back for surprises like delivery fees or a lamp that doesn't work out.

FAQ

Should I spend most on the sofa?

Usually, yes. It's the most-used and hardest-to-replace piece in a living room. Accessories are easy to upgrade later.

What's a contingency for?

Delivery charges, assembly, a returned item or a price change. Ten percent is a sensible minimum.

Can a designer save money?

Often, by avoiding costly mistakes and accessing trade pricing, but compare their fee against your budget.
