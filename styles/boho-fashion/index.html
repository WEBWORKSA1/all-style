Bohemian

Free-spirited layers, prints and natural textures.

freeearthyromantic
Get a personal stylist for this style

Also called: boho, boho chic, bohemian style

What is Bohemian?

Boho fashion layers flowing silhouettes, earthy prints and handcrafted accessories. It feels relaxed, romantic and well-travelled, mixing vintage, folk and festival references.

Origins. Draws on 19th-century artistic bohemians and 1960s–70s counterculture, revived by festival fashion in the 2000s.

The palette

Tap a colour to copy its hex code.

Signature pieces

  • Maxi dress or tiered skirt
  • Peasant blouse
  • Fringed suede jacket
  • Wide-brim hat
  • Layered necklaces and stacked rings
  • Ankle boots or sandals

Fabrics & materials

  • Cotton gauze
  • Crochet
  • Suede
  • Linen
  • Embroidered fabrics

Do

  • Mix prints that share a colour
  • Add handmade or vintage accessories
  • Keep one element fitted for balance

Don't

  • Wear costume-like festival pieces daily
  • Pile on every accessory at once

Start in three steps

  1. Start with a flowing midi or maxi dress
  2. Add a suede or denim layer
  3. Finish with one artisanal piece of jewellery

Who it suits & budget tip

Creative, outdoorsy people who like comfort with personality.

Budget tip: Vintage and second-hand shops are full of boho staples.

Frequently asked questions

What is Bohemian?

Boho fashion layers flowing silhouettes, earthy prints and handcrafted accessories. It feels relaxed, romantic and well-travelled, mixing vintage, folk and festival references.

What colours go with Bohemian?

A typical Bohemian palette uses terracotta (#C0673F), mustard (#D6A740), sage (#9AAE8C), cream (#F2E8D5) and rust.

How do I start with Bohemian?

1. Start with a flowing midi or maxi dress. 2. Add a suede or denim layer. 3. Finish with one artisanal piece of jewellery.

What styles go well with Bohemian?

Bohemian blends naturally with Cottagecore, Bohemian Interior, Western.

Who suits Bohemian?

Creative, outdoorsy people who like comfort with personality.
