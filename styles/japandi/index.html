Japandi

Japanese calm meets Scandinavian warmth.

serenegroundeduncluttered
Get an interior designer for this style

Also called: japandi, japandi style, japandi interior

What is Japandi?

Japandi blends Japanese minimalism and wabi-sabi with Scandinavian comfort and function. Low furniture, natural wood, muted earth tones and very little clutter create rooms that feel quiet and grounded.

Origins. The name joins 'Japanese' and 'Scandi'; the two traditions share a love of craft, nature and restraint, and the hybrid became widely popular from around 2018.

The palette

Tap a colour to copy its hex code.

Signature elements

  • Low-profile platform bed or sofa
  • Light oak or dark walnut furniture
  • Paper or rattan pendant lights
  • Handmade ceramics
  • One statement plant or branch
  • Hidden storage to keep surfaces clear

Materials & finishes

  • Oak and ash
  • Linen
  • Rattan
  • Stone
  • Paper

Do

  • Edit ruthlessly: keep only what you love and use
  • Mix light and dark woods deliberately
  • Choose handmade objects over mass-produced decor

Don't

  • Fill shelves with knick-knacks
  • Use glossy, synthetic finishes

Start in three steps

  1. Clear every surface, then add back three objects
  2. Swap a bright light fixture for a paper or rattan shade
  3. Add one low wooden piece and a linen textile

Who it suits & budget tip

Calm-seekers, small-space dwellers and anyone who loves natural materials.

Budget tip: Buy one well-made wooden piece and pair it with affordable linen and secondhand ceramics.

Frequently asked questions

What is Japandi?

Japandi blends Japanese minimalism and wabi-sabi with Scandinavian comfort and function. Low furniture, natural wood, muted earth tones and very little clutter create rooms that feel quiet and grounded.

What colours go with Japandi?

A typical Japandi palette uses rice paper (#F2EDE3), oak (#C2A27C), charcoal (#3C3A36), clay (#B88E6F) and moss.

How do I start with Japandi?

1. Clear every surface, then add back three objects. 2. Swap a bright light fixture for a paper or rattan shade. 3. Add one low wooden piece and a linen textile.

What styles go well with Japandi?

Japandi blends naturally with Wabi-Sabi, Scandinavian, Minimalist Interior.

Who suits Japandi?

Calm-seekers, small-space dwellers and anyone who loves natural materials.
