Coastal

Breezy whites, sandy neutrals and ocean blues.

airyrelaxedfresh
Get an interior designer for this style

Also called: coastal style, coastal interior, beach house style, coastal decor

What is Coastal?

Coastal interiors feel like a beach house: airy whites, sandy neutrals, soft blues, natural fibres and lots of light. Modern versions skip the anchors and shells for texture and calm.

Origins. Inspired by seaside homes in the US, Mediterranean and Australia; modern 'coastal' design moved away from nautical kitsch in the 2010s.

The palette

Tap a colour to copy its hex code.

Signature elements

  • Slipcovered white sofa
  • Jute or sisal rugs
  • Rattan or wicker chairs
  • Linen curtains
  • Weathered wood
  • Blue-and-white ceramics

Materials & finishes

  • Linen
  • Jute
  • Rattan
  • Whitewashed wood
  • Glass

Do

  • Let in as much light as possible
  • Use natural fibres
  • Keep blues soft

Don't

  • Cover everything in anchors and seashells
  • Use heavy, dark furniture

Start in three steps

  1. Add a jute rug
  2. Swap curtains for light linen
  3. Introduce one soft blue

Who it suits & budget tip

Anyone wanting a calm, vacation feel at home.

Budget tip: Slipcovers transform tired sofas for little money.

Frequently asked questions

What is Coastal?

Coastal interiors feel like a beach house: airy whites, sandy neutrals, soft blues, natural fibres and lots of light. Modern versions skip the anchors and shells for texture and calm.

What colours go with Coastal?

A typical Coastal palette uses white sand (#F5EFE4), driftwood (#BFA98A), sea glass (#9FC3BE), ocean (#3C6E8F) and linen.

How do I start with Coastal?

1. Add a jute rug. 2. Swap curtains for light linen. 3. Introduce one soft blue.

What styles go well with Coastal?

Coastal blends naturally with Coastal Grandmother, Mediterranean, Scandinavian.

Who suits Coastal?

Anyone wanting a calm, vacation feel at home.
