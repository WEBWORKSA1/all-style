Grandmillennial

Granny chic, made fresh: chintz, wicker and scalloped edges.

cheerfulnostalgiccosy
Get an interior designer for this style

Also called: grandmillennial, granny chic, new traditional

What is Grandmillennial?

Grandmillennial reworks grandmother-era decor (chintz, needlepoint, ruffled lampshades, wicker, blue-and-white porcelain) with a fresh, youthful eye and a lot of pattern.

Origins. Named in a 2019 article to describe millennials embracing traditional decor.

The palette

Tap a colour to copy its hex code.

Signature elements

  • Floral or chintz fabrics
  • Scalloped and pleated lampshades
  • Wicker and rattan
  • Blue-and-white porcelain
  • Needlepoint pillows
  • Skirted tables

Materials & finishes

  • Chintz cotton
  • Wicker
  • Porcelain
  • Needlepoint
  • Painted wood

Do

  • Mix patterns in a shared palette
  • Use vintage finds
  • Keep it cheerful

Don't

  • Let it look like a museum
  • Ignore modern comfort

Start in three steps

  1. Add a pleated lampshade
  2. Display blue-and-white porcelain
  3. Use one floral fabric

Who it suits & budget tip

Nostalgic decorators and pattern lovers.

Budget tip: Estate sales are packed with grandmillennial pieces.

Frequently asked questions

What is Grandmillennial?

Grandmillennial reworks grandmother-era decor (chintz, needlepoint, ruffled lampshades, wicker, blue-and-white porcelain) with a fresh, youthful eye and a lot of pattern.

What colours go with Grandmillennial?

A typical Grandmillennial palette uses chintz pink (#E7A9A6), porcelain blue (#3E5F9C), leaf green (#7FA36B), buttercream (#F5E7C1) and wicker.

How do I start with Grandmillennial?

1. Add a pleated lampshade. 2. Display blue-and-white porcelain. 3. Use one floral fabric.

What styles go well with Grandmillennial?

Grandmillennial blends naturally with Traditional, Maximalist, French Country.

Who suits Grandmillennial?

Nostalgic decorators and pattern lovers.
