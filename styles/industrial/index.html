Interiors

Industrial

Exposed brick, raw metal and loft-sized drama.

rawurbanbold
Get an interior designer for this style

Also called: industrial style, industrial interior, loft style

What is Industrial?

Industrial interiors celebrate raw structure: exposed brick, ductwork and concrete, paired with metal and reclaimed wood furniture and Edison-bulb lighting.

Origins. Grew from converting old factories and warehouses into lofts, notably in New York from the 1970s onward.

The palette

Tap a colour to copy its hex code.

Signature elements

  • Exposed brick or concrete
  • Metal-frame shelving
  • Leather sofa
  • Edison bulb pendants
  • Reclaimed wood tables
  • Factory-style windows

Materials & finishes

  • Concrete
  • Steel
  • Reclaimed wood
  • Leather
  • Brick

Do

  • Show structure instead of hiding it
  • Warm it up with wood, leather and rugs
  • Use large-scale lighting

Don't

  • Leave it cold with no soft textures
  • Fake it with cheap brick wallpaper

Start in three steps

  1. Add a metal-and-wood shelf
  2. Install an exposed-bulb pendant
  3. Bring in a leather chair

Who it suits & budget tip

City dwellers and lovers of raw materials.

Budget tip: Salvage yards and restaurant-supply stores are goldmines.

Frequently asked questions

What is Industrial?

Industrial interiors celebrate raw structure: exposed brick, ductwork and concrete, paired with metal and reclaimed wood furniture and Edison-bulb lighting.

What colours go with Industrial?

A typical Industrial palette uses concrete (#9A9A96), brick (#8C4A35), steel (#4A5058), leather (#6B4429) and black.

How do I start with Industrial?

1. Add a metal-and-wood shelf. 2. Install an exposed-bulb pendant. 3. Bring in a leather chair.

What styles go well with Industrial?

Industrial blends naturally with Mid-Century Modern, Contemporary, Grunge.

Who suits Industrial?

City dwellers and lovers of raw materials.
