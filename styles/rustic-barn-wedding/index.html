Rustic Barn Wedding

Country charm: wood, string lights and wildflowers.

warmhomeycountry
Get a wedding or event stylist for this style

Also called: rustic wedding, barn wedding, farm wedding

What is Rustic Barn Wedding?

A rustic barn wedding is set in a barn or farm, decorated with wood, burlap or linen, string lights, wildflowers and family-style dining. It feels warm and community-focused.

Origins. Grew popular in North America in the 2000s–2010s as couples sought relaxed, personal venues.

The palette

Tap a colour to copy its hex code.

What it includes

  • Barn or farm venue
  • String lights
  • Long wooden tables
  • Wildflowers in jars
  • Hay bales or wooden benches
  • Family-style food

Fabrics & materials

  • Reclaimed wood
  • Linen
  • Mason jars
  • Wildflowers
  • Lace

Do

  • Plan for weather and comfort
  • Light the space warmly
  • Use local, seasonal flowers

Don't

  • Overdo burlap and mason jars
  • Forget bathrooms and parking

Start in three steps

  1. Book a barn with good facilities
  2. Add string lights
  3. Set long family-style tables

Who it suits & budget tip

Country lovers and couples hosting family-centred celebrations.

Budget tip: Local wildflowers and DIY jars keep costs low.

Frequently asked questions

What is Rustic Barn Wedding?

A rustic barn wedding is set in a barn or farm, decorated with wood, burlap or linen, string lights, wildflowers and family-style dining. It feels warm and community-focused.

What colours go with Rustic Barn Wedding?

A typical Rustic Barn Wedding palette uses barn wood (#8A6A4C), wildflower (#D9A6C4), sage (#9FAF8E), cream (#F3EBDB) and dusty blue.

How do I start with Rustic Barn Wedding?

1. Book a barn with good facilities. 2. Add string lights. 3. Set long family-style tables.

What styles go well with Rustic Barn Wedding?

Rustic Barn Wedding blends naturally with Modern Farmhouse, Rustic, Boho Wedding.

Who suits Rustic Barn Wedding?

Country lovers and couples hosting family-centred celebrations.
