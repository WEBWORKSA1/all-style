Sprezzatura (Italian Menswear)

Tailored menswear worn with studied nonchalance.

Also called: sprezzatura, italian menswear, pitti style

What is Sprezzatura (Italian Menswear)?

Sprezzatura is the Italian art of making great tailoring look effortless: soft jackets, open collars, rolled sleeves and a slightly imperfect tie. It rewards fit, texture and confidence.

Origins. The word comes from a 1528 Italian book on courtly manners; in menswear it became shorthand for Neapolitan and Florentine style, especially around the Pitti Uomo trade fair.

  • Cotton
  • Cashmere

Do

  • Get the fit right first
  • Mix textures within one palette
  • Leave one detail slightly imperfect

Don't

  • Overdo 'imperfection' until it looks sloppy
  • Wear stiff, padded suits

Start in three steps

  1. Find an unstructured blazer
  2. Pair with pleated trousers and an open-collar shirt
  3. Add suede loafers

Who it suits & budget tip

Men who want tailored style that feels relaxed.

Budget tip: Unstructured jackets are easy to find second-hand and alter.

What colours go with Sprezzatura (Italian Menswear)?

A typical Sprezzatura (Italian Menswear) palette uses tobacco (#7A5532), navy (#1E2A44), ecru (#EFE6D4), olive (#6B6B3A) and sky blue.
