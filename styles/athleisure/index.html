Athleisure

Gym-ready pieces styled for everything else.

comfortableactivemodern
Get a personal stylist for this style

Also called: athleisure, sporty chic, activewear style

What is Athleisure?

Athleisure wears performance fabrics outside the gym: leggings, joggers, technical layers and sneakers styled with polished pieces like blazers or long coats.

Origins. Rose through the 2010s as stretch activewear became comfortable and stylish enough for daily wear.

The palette

Tap a colour to copy its hex code.

Signature pieces

  • High-rise leggings or joggers
  • Fitted performance top
  • Zip-up or crewneck sweatshirt
  • Clean running sneakers
  • Long wool coat to dress it up
  • Belt bag

Fabrics & materials

  • Technical jersey
  • Nylon-elastane
  • Cotton fleece
  • Mesh
  • Recycled polyester

Do

  • Keep sets matching or tonal
  • Add one tailored piece for contrast
  • Keep sneakers spotless

Don't

  • Wear faded or stretched gym kit out
  • Pair with formal shoes awkwardly

Start in three steps

  1. Buy a matching tonal set
  2. Add a long coat
  3. Finish with clean sneakers

Who it suits & budget tip

Busy, active people who live in comfortable clothes.

Budget tip: Mid-range activewear performs well; pay more only for fit.

Frequently asked questions

What is Athleisure?

Athleisure wears performance fabrics outside the gym: leggings, joggers, technical layers and sneakers styled with polished pieces like blazers or long coats.

What colours go with Athleisure?

A typical Athleisure palette uses black (#121212), heather grey (#B5B5B5), white (#F8F8F8), mocha (#8B6F5A) and sage.

How do I start with Athleisure?

1. Buy a matching tonal set. 2. Add a long coat. 3. Finish with clean sneakers.

What styles go well with Athleisure?

Athleisure blends naturally with Streetwear, Gorpcore, Scandi Style.

Who suits Athleisure?

Busy, active people who live in comfortable clothes.
