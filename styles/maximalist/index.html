Maximalist

More is more: colour, pattern and personality.

exuberantpersonalbold
Get an interior designer for this style

Also called: maximalism, maximalist decor, more is more

What is Maximalist?

Maximalism layers bold colour, clashing patterns, art and collections to create rooms full of personality. Done well, a strong palette and repeated motifs hold the chaos together.

Origins. A reaction against minimalism, drawing on Victorian collecting and bold 1970s and 1980s interiors; surged in the late 2010s.

The palette

Tap a colour to copy its hex code.

Signature elements

  • Gallery walls
  • Pattern on pattern (wallpaper, rugs, upholstery)
  • Bold painted ceilings
  • Collections on display
  • Velvet and fringe
  • Statement lighting

Materials & finishes

  • Velvet
  • Wallpaper
  • Lacquer
  • Brass
  • Ceramics

Do

  • Repeat colours across patterns to unify
  • Display what you love
  • Use scale variation

Don't

  • Forget a focal point
  • Mistake clutter for curation

Start in three steps

  1. Paint one wall a saturated colour
  2. Build a gallery wall
  3. Mix two patterns that share a colour

Who it suits & budget tip

Collectors and colour lovers.

Budget tip: Paint and thrifted art give maximal impact cheaply.

Frequently asked questions

What is Maximalist?

Maximalism layers bold colour, clashing patterns, art and collections to create rooms full of personality. Done well, a strong palette and repeated motifs hold the chaos together.

What colours go with Maximalist?

A typical Maximalist palette uses fuchsia (#C2185B), emerald (#1B7F5B), tangerine (#F28C28), cobalt (#1F4FBF) and gold.

How do I start with Maximalist?

1. Paint one wall a saturated colour. 2. Build a gallery wall. 3. Mix two patterns that share a colour.

What styles go well with Maximalist?

Maximalist blends naturally with Art Deco, Bohemian Interior, Grandmillennial.

Who suits Maximalist?

Collectors and colour lovers.
