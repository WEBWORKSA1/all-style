Textured Crop

Short, choppy top with a short fringe: low effort, modern shape.

moderneasysharp
Get a hair & makeup pro for this style

Also called: textured crop, french crop, crop haircut

What is Textured Crop?

The textured crop keeps hair short with a choppy, textured top pushed forward into a short fringe, usually with faded or tapered sides. It hides a receding hairline well and takes seconds to style.

Origins. Evolved from the classic French crop and Caesar cut, popular since the 2010s.

The palette

Tap a colour to copy its hex code.

Defining features

  • Short choppy top
  • Short forward fringe
  • Faded or tapered sides
  • Point-cut texture
  • Matte finish
  • Low maintenance

Tools & products

  • Matte clay
  • Sea-salt spray
  • Texture powder
  • Fine comb
  • Light hairspray

Do

  • Use matte products
  • Rough-dry forward
  • Pair with a skin or low fade

Don't

  • Over-product it into a helmet
  • Let the fringe grow into your eyes

Start in three steps

  1. Ask for a textured crop with a low fade
  2. Apply clay to dry hair
  3. Push forward with fingers

Who it suits & budget tip

Straight or wavy hair, thinning hairlines, oval and square faces.

Budget tip: One tub of clay lasts months.

Frequently asked questions

What is Textured Crop?

The textured crop keeps hair short with a choppy, textured top pushed forward into a short fringe, usually with faded or tapered sides. It hides a receding hairline well and takes seconds to style.

What colours go with Textured Crop?

A typical Textured Crop palette uses dark brown (#3B2B22), black (#141414), light brown (#8A6A4E), ash blonde (#B9A98C) and auburn.

How do I start with Textured Crop?

1. Ask for a textured crop with a low fade. 2. Apply clay to dry hair. 3. Push forward with fingers.

What styles go well with Textured Crop?

Textured Crop blends naturally with Taper Fade, Buzz Cut, Streetwear.

Who suits Textured Crop?

Straight or wavy hair, thinning hairlines, oval and square faces.
