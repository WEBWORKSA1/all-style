Business Casual

Professional without the full suit.

capableneatapproachable
Get a personal stylist for this style

Also called: business casual, office style, workwear

What is Business Casual?

Business casual blends tailored pieces with relaxed ones: trousers or skirts with knitwear, shirts or blouses, and smart shoes. It signals competence while staying comfortable for long days.

Origins. Spread through US offices in the 1990s as 'casual Friday' grew into everyday policy.

The palette

Tap a colour to copy its hex code.

Signature pieces

  • Tailored trousers or chinos
  • Knit polo or fine-gauge sweater
  • Button-down shirt or blouse
  • Unstructured blazer
  • Loafers, derbies or block heels
  • Leather bag

Fabrics & materials

  • Wool blends
  • Cotton twill
  • Merino
  • Ponte
  • Leather

Do

  • Keep fits tailored
  • Choose neutral bases with one colour
  • Match the formality of your workplace

Don't

  • Wear athletic sneakers unless your office allows it
  • Show logos or slogans

Start in three steps

  1. Buy two pairs of well-fitting trousers
  2. Add three knit tops
  3. Choose one versatile blazer

Who it suits & budget tip

Office workers and anyone with client meetings.

Budget tip: A tailor turns budget trousers into expensive-looking ones.

Frequently asked questions

What is Business Casual?

Business casual blends tailored pieces with relaxed ones: trousers or skirts with knitwear, shirts or blouses, and smart shoes. It signals competence while staying comfortable for long days.

What colours go with Business Casual?

A typical Business Casual palette uses navy (#24324F), grey (#8C8C8C), white (#F9F9F9), camel (#BF9B6F) and light blue.

How do I start with Business Casual?

1. Buy two pairs of well-fitting trousers. 2. Add three knit tops. 3. Choose one versatile blazer.

What styles go well with Business Casual?

Business Casual blends naturally with Classic, Smart Casual, Minimalist.

Who suits Business Casual?

Office workers and anyone with client meetings.
