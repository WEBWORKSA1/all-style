Techwear

Black, waterproof, strapped and ready for the city of the future.

futuristicfunctionaldark
Get a personal stylist for this style

Also called: techwear, urban techwear, functional fashion

What is Techwear?

Techwear combines performance fabrics with utilitarian design: waterproof shells, cargo pants with straps and buckles, and a mostly black palette. Function and a futuristic look matter equally.

Origins. Developed from technical outerwear and Japanese and German urban labels in the 2000s–2010s.

The palette

Tap a colour to copy its hex code.

Signature pieces

  • Waterproof shell
  • Cargo trousers with straps
  • Technical sneakers or boots
  • Sling or chest bag
  • Face mask or snood
  • Modular layers

Fabrics & materials

  • Waterproof membranes
  • Ripstop
  • Cordura
  • Softshell
  • Reflective trims

Do

  • Keep the palette monochrome
  • Prioritise real function
  • Layer different technical textures

Don't

  • Add straps that do nothing
  • Mix in loud prints

Start in three steps

  1. Buy one black technical shell
  2. Add tapered cargo pants
  3. Finish with technical sneakers

Who it suits & budget tip

Urban commuters, cyclists and sci-fi fans.

Budget tip: Military surplus offers functional pieces cheaply.

Frequently asked questions

What is Techwear?

Techwear combines performance fabrics with utilitarian design: waterproof shells, cargo pants with straps and buckles, and a mostly black palette. Function and a futuristic look matter equally.

What colours go with Techwear?

A typical Techwear palette uses black (#0D0D0D), gunmetal (#3A3F44), olive drab (#4B5320), ash (#7D7F80) and reflective silver.

How do I start with Techwear?

1. Buy one black technical shell. 2. Add tapered cargo pants. 3. Finish with technical sneakers.

What styles go well with Techwear?

Techwear blends naturally with Streetwear, Gorpcore, Athleisure.

Who suits Techwear?

Urban commuters, cyclists and sci-fi fans.
