Smart Casual

Relaxed, but clearly made an effort.

relaxedneatmodern
Get a wedding or event stylist for this style

Also called: smart casual, dressy casual

What is Smart Casual?

Smart casual pairs relaxed items with sharper ones: chinos or dark jeans with a blazer or knit, a blouse with tailored trousers, clean leather sneakers or loafers. It's the most common (and most confusing) dress code.

Origins. Emerged in the late 20th century as workplaces and social events relaxed formal codes.

The palette

Tap a colour to copy its hex code.

What it includes

  • Dark jeans or chinos
  • Blazer or overshirt
  • Knitwear or polo
  • Leather sneakers or loafers
  • Midi skirt or tailored trousers
  • Simple leather belt

Fabrics & materials

  • Denim
  • Cotton twill
  • Merino
  • Suede
  • Linen

Do

  • Pair one smart piece with one casual piece
  • Make sure everything fits
  • Keep shoes clean

Don't

  • Wear gym wear or ripped jeans
  • Overdress in a full suit and tie

Start in three steps

  1. Start with dark jeans
  2. Add a blazer or knit
  3. Finish with loafers or clean leather sneakers

Who it suits & budget tip

Dinners, dates, creative offices and many events.

Budget tip: Most of the look is pieces you already own; add one blazer.

Frequently asked questions

What is Smart Casual?

Smart casual pairs relaxed items with sharper ones: chinos or dark jeans with a blazer or knit, a blouse with tailored trousers, clean leather sneakers or loafers. It's the most common (and most confusing) dress code.

What colours go with Smart Casual?

A typical Smart Casual palette uses navy (#24304A), stone (#CFC5B4), white (#FAFAF7), olive (#6F6F44) and tan.

How do I start with Smart Casual?

1. Start with dark jeans. 2. Add a blazer or knit. 3. Finish with loafers or clean leather sneakers.

What styles go well with Smart Casual?

Smart Casual blends naturally with Business Casual, Sprezzatura (Italian Menswear), Parisian Chic.

Who suits Smart Casual?

Dinners, dates, creative offices and many events.
