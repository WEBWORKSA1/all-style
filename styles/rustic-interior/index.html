Rustic

Rough-hewn wood, stone and fireside comfort.

warmruggednatural
Get an interior designer for this style

Also called: rustic style, rustic interior, cabin style, lodge style

What is Rustic?

Rustic interiors celebrate raw, natural materials: rough wood beams, stone fireplaces, leather and wool, in warm earthy colours. It feels like a mountain cabin.

Origins. Inspired by rural cabins, lodges and farmhouses.

The palette

Tap a colour to copy its hex code.

Signature elements

  • Exposed wood beams
  • Stone fireplace
  • Leather armchairs
  • Plaid and wool throws
  • Antler-free natural decor
  • Chunky wood tables

Materials & finishes

  • Rough wood
  • Stone
  • Leather
  • Wool
  • Iron

Do

  • Show natural imperfections
  • Layer cosy textiles
  • Keep colours earthy

Don't

  • Overdo hunting-lodge clichés
  • Make it dark without enough lighting

Start in three steps

  1. Add a chunky wood piece
  2. Layer a wool or plaid throw
  3. Bring in a leather chair

Who it suits & budget tip

Outdoorsy people and cosy-cabin dreamers.

Budget tip: Reclaimed wood and secondhand leather fit perfectly.

Frequently asked questions

What is Rustic?

Rustic interiors celebrate raw, natural materials: rough wood beams, stone fireplaces, leather and wool, in warm earthy colours. It feels like a mountain cabin.

What colours go with Rustic?

A typical Rustic palette uses pine (#A0784C), stone (#8C877E), forest (#3D4F37), rust (#9C4A2B) and cream wool.

How do I start with Rustic?

1. Add a chunky wood piece. 2. Layer a wool or plaid throw. 3. Bring in a leather chair.

What styles go well with Rustic?

Rustic blends naturally with Modern Farmhouse, Wabi-Sabi, Rustic Barn Wedding.

Who suits Rustic?

Outdoorsy people and cosy-cabin dreamers.
