Mid-Century Modern

1950s optimism: tapered legs, teak and clean lines.

optimisticretrofunctional
Get an interior designer for this style

Also called: mid-century modern, midcentury modern, mcm

What is Mid-Century Modern?

Mid-Century Modern furniture is defined by organic and geometric shapes, tapered wooden legs, moulded plywood and a mix of warm woods with pops of mustard, teal or orange. It feels both retro and surprisingly current.

Origins. Design from roughly 1945–1969, shaped by American and Scandinavian designers and new manufacturing techniques.

The palette

Tap a colour to copy its hex code.

Signature elements

  • Sofa or chairs with tapered legs
  • Low credenza
  • Starburst or globe lighting
  • Moulded plastic or plywood chairs
  • Geometric rugs
  • Statement lounge chair

Materials & finishes

  • Teak and walnut
  • Moulded plywood
  • Brass
  • Wool
  • Fibreglass

Do

  • Mix vintage originals with modern reproductions
  • Use colour in accents
  • Keep layouts open and low

Don't

  • Make every piece matching retro (it becomes a film set)
  • Crowd the room

Start in three steps

  1. Add one piece with tapered legs
  2. Choose a globe or sputnik light
  3. Bring in one mustard or teal accent

Who it suits & budget tip

Design lovers, collectors and retro fans.

Budget tip: Online secondhand marketplaces are full of real mid-century pieces.

Frequently asked questions

What is Mid-Century Modern?

Mid-Century Modern furniture is defined by organic and geometric shapes, tapered wooden legs, moulded plywood and a mix of warm woods with pops of mustard, teal or orange. It feels both retro and surprisingly current.

What colours go with Mid-Century Modern?

A typical Mid-Century Modern palette uses teak (#9C5B2E), mustard (#D9A520), teal (#2F7F86), burnt orange (#C8642C) and cream.

How do I start with Mid-Century Modern?

1. Add one piece with tapered legs. 2. Choose a globe or sputnik light. 3. Bring in one mustard or teal accent.

What styles go well with Mid-Century Modern?

Mid-Century Modern blends naturally with Scandinavian, Contemporary, Modern Organic.

Who suits Mid-Century Modern?

Design lovers, collectors and retro fans.
