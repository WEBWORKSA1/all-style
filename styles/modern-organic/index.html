Modern Organic

Warm minimalism: curves, wood and natural texture.

warmtactilecalm
Get an interior designer for this style

Also called: modern organic, organic modern, warm minimalism

What is Modern Organic?

Modern Organic combines minimalist layouts with natural materials, soft curves and warm neutrals. Think limewash walls, bouclé, travertine and sculptural wood.

Origins. Rose in the late 2010s as a warmer, more tactile answer to cool minimalism.

The palette

Tap a colour to copy its hex code.

Signature elements

  • Curved bouclé seating
  • Travertine or stone tables
  • Limewash walls
  • Organic-shaped mirrors
  • Large branches or olive trees
  • Woven baskets

Materials & finishes

  • Bouclé
  • Travertine
  • Walnut
  • Limewash
  • Jute

Do

  • Favour curves
  • Layer natural textures
  • Keep colours warm and earthy

Don't

  • Introduce shiny chrome
  • Overload with pattern

Start in three steps

  1. Add a curved piece of furniture
  2. Bring in stone or travertine
  3. Place a large branch in a vase

Who it suits & budget tip

People who like minimalism but want warmth.

Budget tip: Limewash paint is an affordable way to get the texture.

Frequently asked questions

What is Modern Organic?

Modern Organic combines minimalist layouts with natural materials, soft curves and warm neutrals. Think limewash walls, bouclé, travertine and sculptural wood.

What colours go with Modern Organic?

A typical Modern Organic palette uses limewash (#E9E1D3), travertine (#D3C2A5), walnut (#6B4B34), olive (#8A8B62) and clay.

How do I start with Modern Organic?

1. Add a curved piece of furniture. 2. Bring in stone or travertine. 3. Place a large branch in a vase.

What styles go well with Modern Organic?

Modern Organic blends naturally with Japandi, Contemporary, Wabi-Sabi.

Who suits Modern Organic?

People who like minimalism but want warmth.
