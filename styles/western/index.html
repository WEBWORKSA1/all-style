Western

Cowboy boots, denim and turquoise, styled for the city.

ruggedfreesun-baked
Get a personal stylist for this style

Also called: western style, cowboy core, coastal cowgirl, westerncore

What is Western?

Western style takes ranch and rodeo staples (boots, denim, fringe, big buckles and turquoise) and wears them as fashion. It ranges from rugged to glamorous.

Origins. Rooted in 19th-century American ranch wear, repeatedly revived by music and film, including a major comeback from 2023.

Fabrics & materials

  • Denim

Do

  • Pick one western hero piece per look
  • Pair boots with modern basics
  • Use authentic leather

Don't

  • Wear a full costume with hat, chaps and spurs
  • Buy cheap plastic 'leather' boots

Start in three steps

  1. Add cowboy boots to jeans and a tee
  2. Try a snap shirt
  3. Add a turquoise ring

Who it suits & budget tip

Adventurous dressers who like a rugged edge.

Budget tip: Vintage boots are often better made than new fast-fashion versions.

Frequently asked questions

What is Western?

Western style takes ranch and rodeo staples (boots, denim, fringe, big buckles and turquoise) and wears them as fashion. It ranges from rugged to glamorous.

What colours go with Western?

A typical Western palette uses denim (#3E5C80), saddle brown (#8B5A2B), turquoise (#3FB6A8), bone (#EDE4D3) and rust.

How do I start with Western?

1. Add cowboy boots to jeans and a tee. 2. Try a snap shirt. 3. Add a turquoise ring.

What styles go well with Western?

Western blends naturally with Bohemian, Rustic, Rustic Barn Wedding.

Who suits Western?

Adventurous dressers who like a rugged edge.
