Clean Girl

Slick bun, glowing skin, gold hoops, effortless basics.

freshsleekeffortless
Get a personal stylist for this style

Also called: clean girl aesthetic, clean girl look, that girl

What is Clean Girl?

The Clean Girl aesthetic is about looking fresh and put-together with minimal effort: slicked-back hair, dewy skin, simple gold jewellery and neutral, fitted basics.

Origins. Went viral on TikTok around 2022, drawing on long-standing beauty looks; it has been critiqued for ignoring the communities that originated some of its signatures.

Fabrics & materials

  • Ribbed cotton

  • Gold-tone metal

Do

  • Focus on skincare and grooming
  • Keep colours neutral
  • Pick a few signature accessories

Don't

  • Use heavy makeup
  • Mix busy prints

Start in three steps

  1. Master a sleek bun
  2. Add gold hoops
  3. Wear a neutral fitted top with relaxed trousers

Who it suits & budget tip

People who like a low-maintenance, polished look.

Frequently asked questions

What is Clean Girl?

The Clean Girl aesthetic is about looking fresh and put-together with minimal effort: slicked-back hair, dewy skin, simple gold jewellery and neutral, fitted basics.

What colours go with Clean Girl?

A typical Clean Girl palette uses milk (#F7F3EC), latte (#C9AE8F), gold (#C9A24D), soft white (#FFFDF8) and taupe.

How do I start with Clean Girl?

1. Master a sleek bun. 2. Add gold hoops. 3. Wear a neutral fitted top with relaxed trousers.

What styles go well with Clean Girl?

Clean Girl blends naturally with Minimalist, No-Makeup Makeup, Athleisure.

Who suits Clean Girl?

People who like a low-maintenance, polished look.
