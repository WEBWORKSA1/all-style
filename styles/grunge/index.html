Grunge

Flannel, combat boots and a deliberate lack of polish.

rawrebelliouslaid-back
Get a personal stylist for this style

Also called: grunge fashion, grunge style, soft grunge

What is Grunge?

Grunge is anti-fashion: worn flannel, ripped denim, band tees and boots layered with an undone attitude. The point is comfort and rebellion rather than perfection.

Origins. Came out of the Seattle music scene of the late 1980s and early 1990s and was famously put on a runway in 1993.

Frequently asked questions

What is Grunge?

Grunge is anti-fashion: worn flannel, ripped denim, band tees and boots layered with an undone attitude. The point is comfort and rebellion rather than perfection.

What colours go with Grunge?

A typical Grunge palette uses black (#141414), plaid red (#8C2A2A), olive (#5B5E3A), washed denim (#5D7391) and charcoal.

How do I start with Grunge?

1. Throw a flannel over a dark tee. 2. Swap trainers for combat boots. 3. Add a beanie.

What styles go well with Grunge?

Grunge blends naturally with Streetwear, Y2K, Industrial.

Who suits Grunge?

Music fans and people who hate dressing up.
