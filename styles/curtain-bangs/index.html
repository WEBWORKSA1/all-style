Curtain Bangs

A soft, centre-parted fringe that frames the face.

softromanticflattering
Get a hair & makeup pro for this style

Also called: curtain bangs, curtain fringe

What is Curtain Bangs?

Curtain bangs are longer at the sides and shorter in the middle, parted down the centre so they sweep away like curtains. They soften features and grow out gracefully.

Origins. Iconic in the late 1960s and 1970s, revived from around 2020 as a low-commitment fringe.

The palette

Tap a colour to copy its hex code.

Defining features

  • Centre part
  • Shorter middle pieces
  • Longer, sweeping sides
  • Blends into face-framing layers
  • Round-brush styling
  • Works with any length

Tools & products

  • Round brush
  • Velcro rollers
  • Lightweight heat protectant
  • Dry shampoo
  • Flexible hairspray

Do

  • Style them while slightly damp
  • Use a round brush to sweep them outward
  • Keep them at cheekbone length

Don't

  • Cut them too short initially
  • Let them sit flat and greasy: use dry shampoo

Start in three steps

  1. Ask for cheekbone-length curtain bangs
  2. Blow-dry with a round brush
  3. Set with a Velcro roller

Who it suits & budget tip

Most face shapes, especially long, heart and square faces.

Budget tip: Bang trims are often cheap or free between appointments.

Frequently asked questions

What is Curtain Bangs?

Curtain bangs are longer at the sides and shorter in the middle, parted down the centre so they sweep away like curtains. They soften features and grow out gracefully.

What colours go with Curtain Bangs?

A typical Curtain Bangs palette uses chestnut (#6B3E26), caramel (#B07A47), blonde (#D9B97A), dark brown (#3B2A21) and auburn.

How do I start with Curtain Bangs?

1. Ask for cheekbone-length curtain bangs. 2. Blow-dry with a round brush. 3. Set with a Velcro roller.

What styles go well with Curtain Bangs?

Curtain Bangs blends naturally with Wolf Cut, Shag, Butterfly Cut.

Who suits Curtain Bangs?

Most face shapes, especially long, heart and square faces.
