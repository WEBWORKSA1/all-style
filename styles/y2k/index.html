Wear

Y2K

Low-rise, baby tees and shiny everything: the 2000s, remixed.

playfulshinynostalgic
Get a personal stylist for this style

Also called: y2k fashion, y2k aesthetic, 2000s fashion

What is Y2K?

Y2K revives turn-of-the-millennium fashion: low-rise jeans, baby tees, butterfly clips, tinted sunglasses and metallics. It is playful, a little kitsch and pop-culture driven.

Origins. Originally late-1990s to mid-2000s pop and tech optimism; revived by Gen Z on social media from around 2020.

The palette

Tap a colour to copy its hex code.

Signature pieces

  • Low-rise or flared jeans
  • Baby tee or halter top
  • Mini skirt
  • Platform sneakers or sandals
  • Tinted rimless sunglasses
  • Mini shoulder bag

Fabrics & materials

  • Stretch denim
  • Satin
  • Velour
  • Mesh
  • Metallic fabrics

Do

  • Pick one or two 2000s signatures per outfit
  • Play with colour and shine
  • Have fun with accessories

Don't

  • Recreate a full 2003 look head to toe
  • Ignore fit and comfort

Start in three steps

  1. Add a baby tee to your usual jeans
  2. Try a mini shoulder bag
  3. Experiment with tinted glasses

Who it suits & budget tip

Trend-lovers and anyone with a playful streak.

Budget tip: Thrift stores still hold real 2000s pieces.

Frequently asked questions

What is Y2K?

Y2K revives turn-of-the-millennium fashion: low-rise jeans, baby tees, butterfly clips, tinted sunglasses and metallics. It is playful, a little kitsch and pop-culture driven.

What colours go with Y2K?

A typical Y2K palette uses baby pink (#F7B6D2), chrome (#C9CED6), baby blue (#A9D3F5), lime (#C6F04D) and lilac.

How do I start with Y2K?

1. Add a baby tee to your usual jeans. 2. Try a mini shoulder bag. 3. Experiment with tinted glasses.

What styles go well with Y2K?

Y2K blends naturally with Streetwear, Coquette, Grunge.

Who suits Y2K?

Trend-lovers and anyone with a playful streak.
