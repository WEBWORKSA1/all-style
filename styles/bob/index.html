Hair & Beauty

Bob

The chin-length classic, from blunt to French.

chiccleanconfident
Get a hair & makeup pro for this style

Also called: bob haircut, french bob, blunt bob, lob

What is Bob?

A bob is cut around the jaw to the shoulders, usually with little layering. Variations include the blunt bob, the French bob (with a fringe, cheekbone to jaw length) and the long bob or 'lob'.

Origins. Became a symbol of independence in the 1920s and has been reinvented every decade since.

The palette

Tap a colour to copy its hex code.

Defining features

  • Jaw-to-shoulder length
  • Blunt or softly bevelled ends
  • Optional fringe (French bob)
  • Side or centre part
  • Sleek or tousled finish
  • Little layering

Tools & products

  • Smoothing cream
  • Flat iron
  • Texture spray
  • Shine serum
  • Paddle brush

Do

  • Match length to your jawline
  • Choose blunt ends for fine hair
  • Get trims every 6–8 weeks

Don't

  • Over-layer fine hair
  • Skip heat protection when straightening

Start in three steps

  1. Decide on length: jaw, chin or collarbone
  2. Choose blunt or textured ends
  3. Pick sleek or tousled styling

Who it suits & budget tip

Fine to medium hair; oval, heart and square faces (lengths adjusted).

Budget tip: A great bob needs frequent trims, so budget for upkeep.

Frequently asked questions

What colours go with Bob?

A typical Bob palette uses jet black (#121212), cool brown (#4D3B33), platinum (#E6E1D6), copper (#B3542A) and mocha.

How do I start with Bob?

1. Decide on length: jaw, chin or collarbone. 2. Choose blunt or textured ends. 3. Pick sleek or tousled styling.

What styles go well with Bob?

Bob blends naturally with Pixie Cut, Curtain Bangs, Parisian Chic.

Who suits Bob?

Fine to medium hair; oval, heart and square faces (lengths adjusted).
