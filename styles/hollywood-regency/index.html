Hollywood Regency

Old Hollywood glamour, high contrast and high shine.

glamorousdramaticluxurious
Get an interior designer for this style

Also called: hollywood regency, hollywood glam, glam interior, glam decor

What is Hollywood Regency?

Hollywood Regency is theatrical luxury: lacquered and mirrored furniture, velvet, bold colours, gold accents and high-contrast patterns like black-and-white stripes.

Origins. Associated with 1930s–50s Hollywood set designers and stars' homes.

The palette

Tap a colour to copy its hex code.

Signature elements

  • Velvet tufted sofa
  • Mirrored or lacquered furniture
  • Gold-framed mirrors
  • Crystal chandeliers
  • Bold wallpaper
  • Animal-print accents

Materials & finishes

  • Velvet
  • Lacquer
  • Mirror
  • Brass
  • Crystal

Do

  • Go for high contrast
  • Choose one bold colour
  • Add shine with metals and mirrors

Don't

  • Make every surface shiny
  • Use cheap-looking plastic gold

Start in three steps

  1. Add a velvet tufted piece
  2. Hang a gold mirror
  3. Use black-and-white pattern

Who it suits & budget tip

Entertainers and drama lovers.

Budget tip: Spray-painted gold frames and thrifted mirrors deliver glam cheaply.

Frequently asked questions

What is Hollywood Regency?

Hollywood Regency is theatrical luxury: lacquered and mirrored furniture, velvet, bold colours, gold accents and high-contrast patterns like black-and-white stripes.

What colours go with Hollywood Regency?

A typical Hollywood Regency palette uses black (#111111), white (#FAFAFA), gold (#CDA349), blush (#E9B8B5) and peacock.

How do I start with Hollywood Regency?

1. Add a velvet tufted piece. 2. Hang a gold mirror. 3. Use black-and-white pattern.

What styles go well with Hollywood Regency?

Hollywood Regency blends naturally with Art Deco, Maximalist, Mob Wife.

Who suits Hollywood Regency?

Entertainers and drama lovers.
