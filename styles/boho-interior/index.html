Bohemian Interior

Plants, pattern and layered global textiles.

eclecticwarmrelaxed
Get an interior designer for this style

Also called: boho interior, bohemian decor, boho decor

What is Bohemian Interior?

Bohemian interiors layer rugs, textiles, plants and collected objects from different cultures and eras. The result is relaxed, colourful and personal.

Origins. Inspired by the free-spirited artists' homes of the 19th and 20th centuries and the 1960s–70s counterculture.

The palette

Tap a colour to copy its hex code.

Signature elements

  • Layered vintage rugs
  • Macramé and woven wall hangings
  • Rattan or cane furniture
  • Floor cushions and poufs
  • Lots of plants
  • Collected art and travel objects

Materials & finishes

  • Rattan
  • Jute
  • Cotton
  • Wool kilims
  • Terracotta

Do

  • Layer textures and patterns in a shared palette
  • Use plants generously
  • Show collected, meaningful objects

Don't

  • Buy it all at once from one store
  • Ignore storage, or it becomes clutter

Start in three steps

  1. Layer a patterned rug over a jute one
  2. Add two big plants
  3. Hang a woven piece

Who it suits & budget tip

Creative, well-travelled people who love colour.

Budget tip: Flea markets and secondhand rugs are the soul of boho.

Frequently asked questions

What is Bohemian Interior?

Bohemian interiors layer rugs, textiles, plants and collected objects from different cultures and eras. The result is relaxed, colourful and personal.

What colours go with Bohemian Interior?

A typical Bohemian Interior palette uses terracotta (#C46A45), saffron (#E0A43A), jungle green (#3E6B48), cream (#F1E7D6) and indigo.

How do I start with Bohemian Interior?

1. Layer a patterned rug over a jute one. 2. Add two big plants. 3. Hang a woven piece.

What styles go well with Bohemian Interior?

Bohemian Interior blends naturally with Bohemian, Biophilic, Maximalist.

Who suits Bohemian Interior?

Creative, well-travelled people who love colour.
