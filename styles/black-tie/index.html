Black Tie

Evening formal: tuxedos and gowns.

elegantformalevening
Get a wedding or event stylist for this style

Also called: black tie, tuxedo, black tie optional

What is Black Tie?

Black tie means a dinner jacket (tuxedo) with a black bow tie for men, and a floor-length gown or very elegant cocktail-length dress for women. 'Black tie optional' allows a dark suit instead.

Origins. The dinner jacket appeared in the 1860s–1880s as a less formal alternative to the tailcoat.

The palette

Tap a colour to copy its hex code.

What it includes

  • Black or midnight dinner jacket with satin lapels
  • Matching trousers with satin stripe
  • White dress shirt and black bow tie
  • Patent or polished black shoes
  • Floor-length or elegant formal gown
  • Evening clutch and fine jewellery

Fabrics & materials

  • Wool barathea
  • Grosgrain or satin silk
  • Silk
  • Velvet (winter)
  • Patent leather

Do

  • Tie a real bow tie if you can
  • Choose rich fabrics for gowns
  • Keep accessories refined

Don't

  • Wear a business suit to strict black tie
  • Choose casual fabrics like cotton jersey

Start in three steps

  1. Check if it's 'black tie' or 'black tie optional'
  2. Get a tuxedo fitted or hired
  3. Plan shoes and outerwear too

Who it suits & budget tip

Galas, formal weddings, award dinners.

Budget tip: A well-fitted owned tuxedo pays off after about three hires.

Frequently asked questions

What is Black Tie?

Black tie means a dinner jacket (tuxedo) with a black bow tie for men, and a floor-length gown or very elegant cocktail-length dress for women. 'Black tie optional' allows a dark suit instead.

What colours go with Black Tie?

A typical Black Tie palette uses black (#101010), midnight navy (#1A2238), ivory (#F5EFE3), emerald (#11573F) and gold.

How do I start with Black Tie?

1. Check if it's 'black tie' or 'black tie optional'. 2. Get a tuxedo fitted or hired. 3. Plan shoes and outerwear too.

What styles go well with Black Tie?

Black Tie blends naturally with White Tie, Cocktail Attire, Classic Elegant Wedding.

Who suits Black Tie?

Galas, formal weddings, award dinners.
