Festive Attire

Cocktail attire with sparkle, colour and seasonal fun.

joyfulsparklingplayful
Get a wedding or event stylist for this style

Also called: festive attire, holiday attire, party attire

What is Festive Attire?

Festive attire invites guests to bring colour, sequins, velvet and playful accessories to holiday and celebration events, usually at cocktail level of formality.

Origins. A modern invitation wording that grew popular in the 2000s for holiday parties and New Year's events.

The palette

Tap a colour to copy its hex code.

What it includes

  • Sequin or velvet piece
  • Jewel-tone suit or dress
  • Festive tie or bow tie
  • Metallic accessories
  • Statement heels or polished boots
  • Seasonal touch (tartan, sparkle)

Fabrics & materials

  • Velvet
  • Sequins
  • Satin
  • Lamé
  • Tartan wool

Do

  • Add one sparkly or seasonal element
  • Stay at cocktail formality
  • Have fun with accessories

Don't

  • Wear novelty jumpers unless invited to
  • Go head-to-toe sequins at a dinner

Start in three steps

  1. Choose one statement fabric
  2. Keep the rest simple
  3. Add metallic accessories

Who it suits & budget tip

Holiday parties and celebrations.

Budget tip: Rent sequins once; buy velvet you can rewear.

Frequently asked questions

What is Festive Attire?

Festive attire invites guests to bring colour, sequins, velvet and playful accessories to holiday and celebration events, usually at cocktail level of formality.

What colours go with Festive Attire?

A typical Festive Attire palette uses velvet green (#1F4D3A), cranberry (#9A1E33), gold sequin (#D2AE52), midnight (#1B1F3B) and silver.

How do I start with Festive Attire?

1. Choose one statement fabric. 2. Keep the rest simple. 3. Add metallic accessories.

What styles go well with Festive Attire?

Festive Attire blends naturally with Cocktail Attire, Maximalist, Mob Wife.

Who suits Festive Attire?

Holiday parties and celebrations.
