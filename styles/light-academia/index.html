Light Academia

Dark Academia's sunnier sibling: linen, cream and poetry in the park.

airygentlecurious
Get a personal stylist for this style

Also called: light academia

What is Light Academia?

Light Academia keeps the scholarly references but swaps the moody palette for cream, beige and soft brown. Linen, cotton and loose knits make it airy and optimistic.

Origins. Grew online alongside Dark Academia from around 2019 as a softer, spring-and-summer counterpart.

The palette

Tap a colour to copy its hex code.

Signature pieces

  • Linen shirt
  • Pleated cream trousers or midi skirt
  • Cardigan or sweater vest
  • Loafers or Mary Janes
  • Canvas tote of books
  • Ribbon or headscarf

Fabrics & materials

  • Linen
  • Cotton
  • Lightweight wool
  • Lace trims
  • Canvas

Do

  • Keep the palette light and warm
  • Choose relaxed but tidy shapes
  • Accessorise with books and simple jewellery

Don't

  • Mix in black or harsh contrast
  • Overdo frills

Start in three steps

  1. Swap one dark knit for a cream cardigan
  2. Add pleated trousers in beige
  3. Carry a canvas tote

Who it suits & budget tip

Romantic, curious types who like a soft, literary look.

Budget tip: Linen is pricey new; vintage linen shirts are plentiful.

Frequently asked questions

What is Light Academia?

Light Academia keeps the scholarly references but swaps the moody palette for cream, beige and soft brown. Linen, cotton and loose knits make it airy and optimistic.

What colours go with Light Academia?

A typical Light Academia palette uses cream (#F4EDE0), beige (#D9C9AE), soft brown (#A68A6D), sage (#B7C2A6) and ivory.

How do I start with Light Academia?

1. Swap one dark knit for a cream cardigan. 2. Add pleated trousers in beige. 3. Carry a canvas tote.

What styles go well with Light Academia?

Light Academia blends naturally with Dark Academia, Cottagecore, Coastal Grandmother.

Who suits Light Academia?

Romantic, curious types who like a soft, literary look.
