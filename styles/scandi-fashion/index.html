Scandi Style

Functional minimalism with a playful Copenhagen twist.

practicalrelaxedfresh
Get a personal stylist for this style

Also called: scandinavian fashion, scandi style, copenhagen style

What is Scandi Style?

Scandi style combines practical, high-quality basics with relaxed silhouettes and occasional bold colour or print. It is designed for bikes, rain and real life, yet always looks considered.

Origins. Associated with Danish and Swedish brands and the street style around Copenhagen Fashion Week from the 2010s.

Signature pieces

  • Oversized blazer

  • Practical rain coat
  • Chunky loafers or clean sneakers
  • Hair clip

Fabrics & materials

  • Wool
  • Organic cotton
  • Denim
  • Recycled nylon
  • Leather

Do

  • Choose comfort first
  • Add one playful colour or print
  • Invest in great outerwear

Don't

  • Wear impractical heels for city days
  • Over-accessorise

Start in three steps

  1. Get an oversized neutral blazer
  2. Wear it with straight jeans and a knit
  3. Add one colour pop

Who it suits & budget tip

Practical people who still want to look current.

Budget tip: Scandi brands resell strongly second-hand, so prices stay fair.

Frequently asked questions

What is Scandi Style?

Scandi style combines practical, high-quality basics with relaxed silhouettes and occasional bold colour or print. It is designed for bikes, rain and real life, yet always looks considered.

What colours go with Scandi Style?

A typical Scandi Style palette uses oat (#E7DFD1), black (#161616), butter yellow (#F3DE8A), grey melange (#A7A7A7) and cobalt.

How do I start with Scandi Style?

1. Get an oversized neutral blazer. 2. Wear it with straight jeans and a knit. 3. Add one colour pop.

What styles go well with Scandi Style?

Scandi Style blends naturally with Minimalist, Scandinavian, Athleisure.

Who suits Scandi Style?

Practical people who still want to look current.
