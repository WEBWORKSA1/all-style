Boho Wedding

Free-spirited weddings: pampas, macramé and golden hour.

freeearthyromantic
Get a wedding or event stylist for this style

Also called: boho wedding, bohemian wedding

What is Boho Wedding?

A boho wedding favours relaxed outdoor settings, flowing dresses, dried and wild flowers, macramé, rugs and lanterns, with an earthy palette and a laid-back timeline.

Origins. Popularised in the 2010s by festival culture and destination weddings.

The palette

Tap a colour to copy its hex code.

What it includes

  • Flowing lace or chiffon dress
  • Dried flowers and pampas grass
  • Macramé backdrops
  • Layered rugs and floor cushions
  • Lanterns and candles
  • Outdoor or barn setting

Fabrics & materials

  • Lace
  • Chiffon
  • Macramé
  • Linen
  • Rattan

Do

  • Choose an outdoor or natural venue
  • Use dried or seasonal flowers
  • Keep the schedule relaxed

Don't

  • Overload with props
  • Ignore guest comfort (shade, seating)

Start in three steps

  1. Pick an earthy 3-colour palette
  2. Choose a natural venue
  3. Plan one macramé or floral focal point

Who it suits & budget tip

Couples who want a relaxed, nature-loving celebration.

Budget tip: Dried flowers can be bought in bulk and reused for decor.

Frequently asked questions

What is Boho Wedding?

A boho wedding favours relaxed outdoor settings, flowing dresses, dried and wild flowers, macramé, rugs and lanterns, with an earthy palette and a laid-back timeline.

What colours go with Boho Wedding?

A typical Boho Wedding palette uses pampas (#E9DCC3), terracotta (#BF6B4A), sage (#A3B18A), rust (#8F3F28) and ivory.

How do I start with Boho Wedding?

1. Pick an earthy 3-colour palette. 2. Choose a natural venue. 3. Plan one macramé or floral focal point.

What styles go well with Boho Wedding?

Boho Wedding blends naturally with Bohemian, Bohemian Interior, Rustic Barn Wedding.

Who suits Boho Wedding?

Couples who want a relaxed, nature-loving celebration.
