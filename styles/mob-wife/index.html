Mob Wife

Faux fur, leopard print, gold and unapologetic glamour.

glamorousloudconfident
Get a personal stylist for this style

Also called: mob wife aesthetic, mob wife

What is Mob Wife?

The Mob Wife aesthetic is loud luxury: big faux-fur coats, animal print, black leather, oversized sunglasses and chunky gold. It is the opposite of quiet luxury.

Origins. Went viral on TikTok in early 2024, inspired by glamorous characters in crime films and TV.

The palette

Tap a colour to copy its hex code.

Signature pieces

  • Faux-fur coat
  • Leopard print
  • Black leather trousers or skirt
  • Oversized sunglasses
  • Chunky gold jewellery
  • Bold red nails

Fabrics & materials

  • Faux fur
  • Leather
  • Satin
  • Lace
  • Gold-tone metal

Do

  • Commit to one statement layer
  • Keep base pieces black
  • Wear it with confidence

Don't

  • Buy real fur unless second-hand and ethical to you
  • Pair with casual athletic wear

Start in three steps

  1. Add a faux-fur coat over black basics
  2. Wear big sunglasses
  3. Stack gold jewellery

Who it suits & budget tip

Bold dressers who enjoy glamour and drama.

Budget tip: Second-hand faux fur and costume gold give maximum impact for little.

Frequently asked questions

What is Mob Wife?

The Mob Wife aesthetic is loud luxury: big faux-fur coats, animal print, black leather, oversized sunglasses and chunky gold. It is the opposite of quiet luxury.

What colours go with Mob Wife?

A typical Mob Wife palette uses black (#0F0F0F), leopard (#B07A3B), gold (#D4AF37), oxblood (#4A0E15) and champagne.

How do I start with Mob Wife?

1. Add a faux-fur coat over black basics. 2. Wear big sunglasses. 3. Stack gold jewellery.

What styles go well with Mob Wife?

Mob Wife blends naturally with Hollywood Regency, Maximalist, Art Deco.

Who suits Mob Wife?

Bold dressers who enjoy glamour and drama.
