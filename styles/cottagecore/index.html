Cottagecore

Pastoral romance: florals, puff sleeves and homemade bread.

pastoralsoftnostalgic
Get a personal stylist for this style

Also called: cottagecore, cottage core

What is Cottagecore?

Cottagecore idealises a slow countryside life. Prairie dresses, florals, aprons and natural fabrics evoke gardens, baking and handcraft.

Origins. Named online around 2018 and boomed in 2020 as people sought comfort and nature during lockdowns.

The palette

Tap a colour to copy its hex code.

Signature pieces

  • Prairie or puff-sleeve dress
  • Floral prints
  • Knitted cardigan
  • Straw basket bag
  • Mary Janes or clogs
  • Hair ribbons

Fabrics & materials

  • Cotton lawn
  • Linen
  • Eyelet lace
  • Wool knit
  • Straw

Do

  • Choose small-scale florals
  • Add handmade touches like embroidery
  • Keep makeup fresh and natural

Don't

  • Wear synthetic, shiny fabrics
  • Make every piece frilly at once

Start in three steps

  1. Find one floral midi dress
  2. Layer a knit cardigan
  3. Add a straw basket

Who it suits & budget tip

Romantics, gardeners and makers.

Budget tip: Sew or upcycle: cottagecore rewards handmade.

Frequently asked questions

What is Cottagecore?

Cottagecore idealises a slow countryside life. Prairie dresses, florals, aprons and natural fabrics evoke gardens, baking and handcraft.

What colours go with Cottagecore?

A typical Cottagecore palette uses meadow green (#8FA77A), blush (#E8C4C0), butter (#F1E2A8), linen white (#F7F2E8) and lavender.

How do I start with Cottagecore?

1. Find one floral midi dress. 2. Layer a knit cardigan. 3. Add a straw basket.

What styles go well with Cottagecore?

Cottagecore blends naturally with Light Academia, Bohemian, French Country.

Who suits Cottagecore?

Romantics, gardeners and makers.
