Transitional

The sweet spot between traditional and contemporary.

balancedtimelesscomfortable
Get an interior designer for this style

Also called: transitional style, transitional interior

What is Transitional?

Transitional design blends traditional comfort with modern simplicity: classic shapes stripped of ornament, neutral palettes and a mix of wood, metal and soft fabrics.

Origins. Became the dominant mainstream style in the late 20th and early 21st century as homeowners wanted timelessness without formality.

The palette

Tap a colour to copy its hex code.

Signature elements

  • Classic-shaped sofa in neutral fabric
  • Mix of wood and metal
  • Simple mouldings
  • Textured neutral rugs
  • Clean-lined lighting
  • Minimal accessories

Materials & finishes

  • Wood
  • Brushed nickel
  • Linen
  • Wool
  • Glass

Do

  • Keep the palette neutral
  • Mix one traditional and one modern element per room
  • Focus on texture

Don't

  • Lean too far either way
  • Use bold patterns everywhere

Start in three steps

  1. Paint in greige
  2. Pair a classic sofa with modern lighting
  3. Add textured throws

Who it suits & budget tip

Anyone who wants a home that never looks dated.

Budget tip: A neutral base lets you change accents cheaply.

Frequently asked questions

What is Transitional?

Transitional design blends traditional comfort with modern simplicity: classic shapes stripped of ornament, neutral palettes and a mix of wood, metal and soft fabrics.

What colours go with Transitional?

A typical Transitional palette uses greige (#C2B9AA), soft white (#F5F3EE), taupe (#8F8273), charcoal (#3D3D3D) and muted blue.

How do I start with Transitional?

1. Paint in greige. 2. Pair a classic sofa with modern lighting. 3. Add textured throws.

What styles go well with Transitional?

Transitional blends naturally with Traditional, Contemporary, Modern Farmhouse.

Who suits Transitional?

Anyone who wants a home that never looks dated.
