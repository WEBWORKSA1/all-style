Old Money

Inherited-looking elegance: logo-free, beautifully made, never in a hurry.

understatedrefinedheritage
Get a personal stylist for this style

Also called: quiet luxury, old money style, stealth wealth

What is Old Money?

Old Money dresses like the clothes were bought decades ago and cared for ever since. Quality fabrics, neutral colours and conservative cuts do the talking; logos, trends and visible effort are avoided. It overlaps with what the internet calls quiet luxury.

Origins. Rooted in East Coast and European upper-class dressing of the mid-20th century (sailing, equestrian and country-club wardrobes), revived online from 2022 by TV costume design and the quiet-luxury conversation.

The palette

Tap a colour to copy its hex code.

Signature pieces

  • Cable-knit or cashmere crewneck
  • Tailored navy blazer
  • Pleated trousers or straight-leg chinos
  • Crisp Oxford or silk shirt
  • Loafers and riding-style boots
  • Simple gold jewellery or a heritage watch

Fabrics & materials

  • Cashmere
  • Merino wool
  • Linen
  • Silk
  • Full-grain leather

Do

  • Buy fewer, better pieces and tailor them
  • Keep colours within navy, camel, cream, grey and deep greens
  • Look after shoes and knitwear visibly well

Don't

  • Wear visible logos or monograms everywhere
  • Chase micro-trends or novelty colours

Start in three steps

  1. Swap one logo item for a plain, well-made equivalent
  2. Get your best trousers and blazer altered to fit
  3. Build outfits around one neutral base: navy or camel

Who it suits & budget tip

Anyone who wants a polished, trend-proof wardrobe that reads expensive without saying so.

Budget tip: Second-hand cashmere and vintage leather goods give the look at a fraction of the price.

Frequently asked questions

What is Old Money?

Old Money dresses like the clothes were bought decades ago and cared for ever since. Quality fabrics, neutral colours and conservative cuts do the talking; logos, trends and visible effort are avoided. It overlaps with what the internet calls quiet luxury.

What colours go with Old Money?

A typical Old Money palette uses navy (#1F2A44), camel (#C19A6B), cream (#F3EBDD), bottle green (#2E4A3A) and burgundy.

How do I start with Old Money?

1. Swap one logo item for a plain, well-made equivalent. 2. Get your best trousers and blazer altered to fit. 3. Build outfits around one neutral base: navy or camel.

What styles go well with Old Money?

Old Money blends naturally with Minimalist, Preppy, Classic.

Who suits Old Money?

Anyone who wants a polished, trend-proof wardrobe that reads expensive without saying so.
