Minimalist Interior

Less, but better: space as the main material.

calmcleanspacious
Get an interior designer for this style

Also called: minimalist interior, minimal home, minimalism decor

What is Minimalist Interior?

Minimalist interiors reduce furniture and decor to the essentials, using clean lines, a restrained palette and generous empty space. Every object is chosen for function or meaning.

Origins. Shaped by modernist architecture, Japanese Zen traditions and 1960s minimal art.

The palette

Tap a colour to copy its hex code.

Signature elements

  • Streamlined sofa
  • Built-in or hidden storage
  • One large artwork
  • Simple lighting
  • Bare surfaces
  • Few, high-quality pieces

Materials & finishes

  • Plaster
  • Wood
  • Stone
  • Steel
  • Wool

Do

  • Invest in storage before decor
  • Choose a tight palette
  • Let architecture show

Don't

  • Make it cold: add texture
  • Buy filler decor

Start in three steps

  1. Remove 30% of objects in one room
  2. Add closed storage
  3. Keep one statement piece

Who it suits & budget tip

People who feel calmer with less.

Budget tip: Minimalism is free to start: subtract first.

Frequently asked questions

What is Minimalist Interior?

Minimalist interiors reduce furniture and decor to the essentials, using clean lines, a restrained palette and generous empty space. Every object is chosen for function or meaning.

What colours go with Minimalist Interior?

A typical Minimalist Interior palette uses white (#FAFAF8), warm grey (#C8C3BB), black (#1B1B1B), oak (#C9A67D) and stone.

How do I start with Minimalist Interior?

1. Remove 30% of objects in one room. 2. Add closed storage. 3. Keep one statement piece.

What styles go well with Minimalist Interior?

Minimalist Interior blends naturally with Japandi, Scandinavian, Contemporary.

Who suits Minimalist Interior?

People who feel calmer with less.
